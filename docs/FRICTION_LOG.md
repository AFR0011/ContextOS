## Trial Rules
- Use ContextOS for one real workday before adding more features.
- Capture every open loop in ContextOS.
- Do not use fallback notes unless ContextOS blocks the work.
- Process the inbox at least twice.
- Use Today for execution.
- Update at least two project pages.
- Use at least one subcontext for a large project, course, assignment, or duty.
- Edit the Dashboard Canvas at least once.
- Add or inspect at least one Resource note.
- Complete at least one daily startup or shutdown review.
- Fix only obvious small bugs found during use.
